About Quorin

A marketing and automation agency where systems converge.

We connect marketing, websites, CRM, and AI automation into one system for realtors, HVAC businesses, and coaches.

The agency
Quorin
Founded 2025

Quorin was founded in 2025 by Khadija Kamran, built on a simple belief: every business that shows up for its customers deserves a system that shows up just as reliably in return.

We believe growth shouldn't come at the cost of the relationships that built it. Every automation, every response, every piece of this system exists to protect something human, the trust a client places in a business the moment they reach out.

That's not a mission statement we keep in a drawer. It's the standard every system we build is measured against, for every client, every time.

The founder
Khadija Kamran
Founder, Quorin, Student of Clinical Psychology

Khadija Kamran is the founder of Quorin, and a student of clinical psychology. She built Quorin after watching talented service business owners lose clients they never knew had reached out, not from lack of skill, but from lack of system.

Most people in this space understand technology. She understands people and technology. Her background in psychology shapes every system she builds, the automations don't just respond fast, they respond right.

"I don't just build systems. I study why they work. When someone reaches out and waits in silence, their brain fills that gap with its own story. That gut feeling decides whether they stay. Every system I build is designed around that gap." Khadija Kamran

Growth should be predictable. A converged system makes it that way, one connected Virtual Office, built and managed end to end, so nothing a business has already earned slips through the cracks.

What we're building toward

Three things we're here to do.

01

Capture every lead

Help HVAC and real estate businesses capture, nurture, and retain more leads through marketing and AI automation, so nothing they've already earned slips away.

02

Build real trust

Help coaches and consultants build a personal brand that actually drives leads, a presence people trust enough to reach out to.

03

One roof, everything connected

Help service-based businesses run marketing, automation, and CRM together, under one system, instead of five disconnected tools.
