Case Study · HVAC · Local Service

AI Client Response & Booking System for an HVAC Service Company

The Situation

A busy HVAC company losing jobs to slow response

An owner-operated HVAC company with a 3-person crew was running on referrals and Google ads and getting inquiries. But the owner was out on jobs all day, couldn't answer every call, and leads would fill out the website form or send a Facebook message and hear nothing for hours. In home services, speed wins. Customers contact 2-3 companies and go with whoever responds first. This company was consistently losing to competitors simply because they weren't responding fast enough.

BEFORE
  • Inquiries sitting in email/Facebook inbox for hours
  • Owner manually quoting every job while on other jobs
  • No follow-up if first message wasn't answered
  • Booking done over the phone, no calendar link
  • Evenings and weekends: zero coverage
  • No way to qualify jobs before spending time on a call
AFTER
  • AI agent responds to every inquiry in under 60 seconds
  • Agent qualifies the job type before owner gets involved
  • Booking link sent automatically to serious inquiries
  • 3-message follow-up sequence if no reply in 24 hours
  • After-hours coverage, system never sleeps
  • Owner only steps in for confirmed, qualified leads
What Was Delivered

An AI response agent trained on the business, GoHighLevel CRM pipeline with full stage tracking, booking automation with calendar integration and reminders, a 3-touch follow-up workflow, a unified inbox pulling Facebook, web form, and SMS into one dashboard, and a full Loom walkthrough so the owner could manage it independently. Built with GoHighLevel, Make, Twilio SMS, Google Calendar, and Calendly.

"I was losing jobs to competitors just because I couldn't answer fast enough when I was on-site. Now the system handles every new inquiry instantly, qualifies the customer, and sends them a booking link before I've even seen the message. It's like having a receptionist who never takes a day off."

Owner, HVAC Service Company · Southeast US

Full CRM & Lead Automation System for a Real Estate Agency

The Problem

A busy agency bleeding leads through manual chaos

A 4-agent real estate team was generating 60–80 leads per month through Facebook ads and a website contact form, but had no system to handle them. Leads were piling up in a shared spreadsheet, follow-up was happening days late (or not at all), and agents had no visibility into where each lead stood. The team was spending 3+ hours daily just on manual data entry and chasing down contact info.

BEFORE
  • Leads stored in a shared Google Sheet with no pipeline
  • Manual SMS/email follow-up, often 24–48 hours late
  • No visibility into which leads were hot vs cold
  • Agents duplicating outreach to the same contact
  • Zero nurture sequence after initial contact
  • Ad leads and web leads managed in separate places
AFTER
  • All leads auto-imported into GoHighLevel pipeline
  • Instant automated SMS + email sent within 2 minutes
  • Color-coded pipeline stages with task reminders
  • Lead deduplication and agent round-robin assignment
  • 7-touch nurture sequence over 14 days
  • Single dashboard, all sources unified
What Was Delivered

A complete lead management infrastructure inside GoHighLevel: a custom pipeline with 7 stages, automated workflows triggered on lead entry, a 14-day SMS and email nurture sequence across 7 touchpoints, Facebook Lead Ads and website form integrations via Make, agent round-robin assignment logic, and a live pipeline value dashboard. Delivered with a full Loom walkthrough so the team could manage it independently.

"We went from chasing leads in a spreadsheet to having everything automated in under two weeks. The response time alone made a noticeable difference. Leads were getting our message before they even finished filling out the form. Best investment we made this quarter."

Agency Owner, Residential Real Estate Team · Texas
